About

Slackgammon is a lightweight single-page backgammon app designed to feel smooth and modern while staying easy to run locally or deploy to static hosting. It includes a built-in engine, installable PWA support, and truly random dice generation in supported browsers.

How to Play

  • Move all 15 of your checkers around the board into your home board, then bear them off. First to bear off all 15 wins.
  • Roll two dice. Each number is a separate move; doubles give you four moves. Use as many dice as legally possible, and use the higher number if only one of two different dice can be played.
  • You may land on an empty point, one occupied by your own checkers, or one occupied by a single opposing checker. Two or more opposing checkers block that point.
  • Landing on a single opposing checker hits it and sends it to the bar. Checkers on the bar must re-enter in the opponent’s home board before any other move.
  • Bear off only after all your active checkers are in your home board. Use an exact roll, or a higher roll only when no checker is on a higher point.

Scoring

A single win scores the cube value. A gammon, when the loser bears off none, scores double; a backgammon, when the loser also has a checker on the bar or in your home board, scores triple. Offer a double before rolling; the other player may accept the higher stakes or drop and concede the game at the current stakes. First to the target score wins. With Crawford enabled, the game after a player reaches one point short is played without the cube.
